## Formats

- **IG carousel:** 6 slides, 1080×1350, brand template (hook → 4 value slides → CTA). Use the carousel template in the content studio.
- **IG reel/TikTok:** hook in first 2 seconds, no intro logos; caption carries the value, video carries the vibe.
- **X thread:** 5–8 tweets; tweet 1 is the hook, last tweet is CTA + link.
- Every post links intent: engagement posts → follow; product posts → zkjverse.com collection link.

## Content pillars (rotate; don't post product-only)

1. The art itself (finished pieces, collection reveals)
2. Process & engineering (how it's made — the differentiator)
3. Dubai architecture & inspiration
4. Story/narrative (Past ∵ Present ⊙ Future ∴ arc, founder journey)
5. Product & drops (limited editions, scarcity stated plainly)

## Rules

- Always apply `zkj-brand-guidelines`; Arabic content also applies `arabic-content`.
- Scarcity messaging must be factual ("10 per variant") — never countdown-timer fake urgency.
- Watermark or handle on every shared artwork image.
- One CTA per post, never two.

## Don'ts

- Don't post the same caption across platforms — adapt per the table above.
- Don't use trending-audio bait unrelated to art/engineering.
- Don't reply to comments in a different register than the post (Arabic post → Arabic replies).

# Arabic Content — ZKJ VERSE Voice

## Language mix (the core rule)

- **Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى)** for information: explanations, facts, product details, body text.
- **Gulf dialect (لهجة خليجية)** for hooks and CTAs: opening lines, questions to the audience, closing calls-to-action.
- Never mix registers inside one sentence.

## Structure

- **Hook first, always.** First line must stop the scroll — a bold claim, a question, or a number. In dialect.
- Short sentences. One idea per line. Whitespace is part of the design.
- End with the brand CTA and handle. Check `ZKJ-IDENTITY.md` for the current CTA line; if absent, use: تابعنا للمزيد @ZKJVERSE

## Typography & formatting

- Use Arabic-compatible fonts in any rendered design: IBM Plex Sans Arabic (body), Noto Kufi Arabic (headlines).
- All rendered layouts must be `dir="rtl"`.
- Numbers: use Western digits (1, 2, 3) — matches the brand's tech aesthetic.
- Hashtags: mix Arabic and English, max 5 (e.g. #فن_رقمي #دبي #NFT #DigitalArt).

## Tone

- Same brand voice as English: confident, artistic, precise — not influencer-hype.
- Culturally grounded in the Gulf/Dubai context; reference the city's architecture and energy naturally.

## Don'ts

- Don't transliterate English words that have common Arabic equivalents.
- Don't write long paragraph blocks — social Arabic content is line-based.
- Don't use Egyptian or Levantine dialect markers; the brand dialect is Gulf.
- Don't translate taglines — `Past ∵ Present ⊙ Future ∴` and `Where Engineering Meets Light` stay in English.

# ZKJ VERSE Brand Guidelines

## Identity

- **Brand:** ZKJ VERSE — premium digital art & photography studio, born in Dubai
- **Sub-brands:** KJ VISION (photography line) · Unknown Presence (digital character collection)
- **Founder handle:** @ZKJVERSE · Instagram @unknown.presence.kj · X @jigikj01
- **Positioning:** where engineering precision meets artistic light — infrastructure, towers, and trippy techno vibes
- **Products:** limited NFTs, premium acrylic & resin prints, smart digital notebooks

## Taglines (use exactly, symbols included)

- `Past ∵ Present ⊙ Future ∴` — the narrative arc
- `Where Engineering Meets Light` — the signature line
- Collection divider: `KJ VISION ◆ UNKNOWN PRESENCE ◆ ZKJ VERSE`

## The narrative (use in About-style copy)

- **Past ∵** — engineering precision met a passion for photography in the heart of Dubai; iconic towers and landmarks through an artistic lens.
- **Present ⊙** — hand-crafted art bridged with digital innovation; NFTs connected to reality through premium acrylic and resin prints; authenticity and creative integrity.
- **Future ∴** — a borderless artistic community; smart digital products; art integrated with the Metaverse as a lifestyle element.

## Voice & tone

- Confident, precise, artistic — an engineer who became an artist. Never salesy or discount-driven.
- Scarcity is real, so state it plainly: "Only 10 pieces per variant will ever be produced." Never fake urgency.
- Bilingual brand: English for the store and international audience, Arabic (see `arabic-content` skill) for regional social content.

## Visual rules

- Dark-first aesthetic: near-black backgrounds, high-contrast white, neon/spectrum accents from the artwork itself.
- The clean white logo sits on black — never recolor it, never place it on busy imagery without a dark overlay.
- Photography style: architectural, geometric, long-exposure light; character art: surreal, masked figures, prismatic color.
- Check `ZKJ-IDENTITY.md` in the project for the current exact hex palette before hardcoding colors; if absent, ask rather than invent.

## Don'ts

- Don't write "ZKJVerse", "Zkj verse", or "the ZKJ" — it is **ZKJ VERSE** (caps) or ZKJ-VERSE in titles.
- Don't describe products as "cheap", "affordable", or "deals" — this is a premium limited-edition brand.
- Don't invent new collection names; the canon is Unknown Presence, KJ Vision, Spectrum Burst.
- Don't use stock-photo aesthetics or generic AI-art look in brand visuals.

What ZKJ SYSTEM is

ZKJ SYSTEM is a publishing dashboard. A brand owner prepares posts, approves them, and the system publishes them at the scheduled time to the brand's own social accounts (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn Company Pages). Only the owner of a brand, or a person the owner invites, can use the dashboard for that brand.

What we store

  • Account details of dashboard users: email address, name, language, and the brand they belong to.
  • Content the brand prepares: titles, captions, media links, schedule, approval decisions and notes.
  • Connection tokens for the social accounts a brand owner connects (OAuth access and refresh tokens, the channel or page id and name, the chosen Pinterest board). Tokens are stored in a server-only database table that no dashboard user or browser can read. They are used only to publish the content the owner approved, to read the resulting post link, and to renew the connection.
  • Publishing results: the link of the published post, the time, and any error message the network returned.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. There is no advertising and no analytics profiling.
  • We do not read your private messages, your subscribers, followers or viewers, and we do not post anything that was not approved in the dashboard.
  • We never ask for or store the password of a social account. Connecting is done on the network's own login page.

Google and YouTube data

ZKJ SYSTEM uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos the owner approved, set their title, description and visibility, and read the channel's name and the uploaded video's link. ZKJ SYSTEM's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. ZKJ SYSTEM also uses YouTube API Services and is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Pinterest and LinkedIn data

With Pinterest we create pins on the board the owner chose and read the account name and boards. With LinkedIn we post to the Company Pages the connected login administers and read the Page names; personal profiles are never used. Access can be revoked from each network's settings; the stored token then stops working and is deleted.

Where the data lives and how long

Data is stored with Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL, EU/US data centres) and the website is served by GitHub Pages. Content and results stay as long as the brand uses the dashboard. Connection tokens are deleted when the owner disconnects a network, when the brand is removed, or on request.

Your rights and contact

Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion at any time: kj@zkjverse.com. We answer within 7 days.

ما نخزّنه

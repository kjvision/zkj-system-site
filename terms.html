1. The service

ZKJ SYSTEM is a dashboard where a brand prepares, approves and schedules its social media content, and where the content is then published to the brand's own connected accounts through each network's official API. Access is by invitation of ZKJ VERSE.

2. Your accounts stay yours

You connect your own YouTube channel, Pinterest account or LinkedIn Company Page on the network's login page. You can disconnect at any time from the dashboard or from the network's settings. You are responsible for your accounts and for the content you approve.

3. Nothing is published without approval

The system publishes only items that were approved in the dashboard, at the scheduled time. Where a network does not allow public publishing by API yet (for example YouTube before Google's audit of the app), the item is uploaded as private and you make it public yourself with one tap.

You confirm that you hold the rights to the media and text you approve and that the content follows the rules of each network. ZKJ VERSE may refuse or remove content that breaks those rules or the law.
